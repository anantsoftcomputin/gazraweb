# Vadvarso image-generation prompt set

All ten images were generated with the built-in image-generation tool as wide, 16:9, photorealistic-natural editorial photographs. The shared direction was: candid documentary photography in Vadodara or a believable western Indian city setting; authentic Indian faces, clothing and skin texture; respectful representation across gender expressions and ages; practical environmental work; natural light; no text, watermarks, dominant flags, tokenism, stereotypes, staged corporate smiles or publicity poses.

1. `01-inclusive-plantation.jpg`: residents, LGBTQIA+ volunteers, students, an older citizen and municipal gardening staff planting native saplings together on an unused urban plot after monsoon rain, with tools, bamboo guards and soil visibly in use.
2. `02-community-contributors.jpg`: a trans woman and municipal gardener reviewing a young-tree care checklist as volunteers water and maintain a neighbourhood green strip, presented as equal collaborators.
3. `03-banyan-legacy.jpg`: a mature, botanically believable banyan canopy with a multigenerational group documenting and caring for a young banyan planted at a respectful distance from buildings and utilities.
4. `04-city-makers.jpg`: a transgender facilitator leading an inclusive civic-planning workshop around a neighbourhood map with residents, students, a wheelchair user, an urban planner and a municipal official.
5. `05-growing-acceptance.jpg`: queer and non-queer neighbours, students and a gardener sharing tools and conversation as they secure one newly planted tree on an ordinary Vadodara neighbourhood verge.
6. `06-inclusive-design.jpg`: a diverse team conducting a site walk, with an urban planner measuring a path, a queer volunteer checking shade, an older resident pointing out seating needs, a wheelchair user testing access and a horticulture worker inspecting soil.
7. `07-pride-responsibility.jpg`: a queer organiser wearing only a subtle rainbow wristband leading a tree-survival audit while volunteers check guards, leaf health, mulch and watering in an established young grove.
8. `08-shared-public-space.jpg`: a lived-in neighbourhood green space used by women, children, students, senior citizens and queer volunteers while municipal gardening work continues, with no single heroic subject.
9. `09-leading-change.jpg`: an LGBTQIA+ coordinator briefing a mixed volunteer team from a hand-drawn site plan before plantation work, with saplings, mulch, gloves and watering cans ready.
10. `10-social-impact-tree.jpg`: an Indian bird among the leaves and figs of a healthy urban tree, with students recording growth, volunteers teaching, a gardener watering and residents talking in the shade below.
