# Vadvarso editorial source notes

These sources informed the ten-part Vadvarso series. They support general historical, environmental, public-space and social claims. They do not establish that a specific Vadvarso site, planting target or municipal partnership has been formally approved.

## Vadodara and the banyan

- [Vadodara Municipal Corporation — History](https://vmc.gov.in/History.aspx): the city’s earlier name, Vadpadraka, and its association with the profuse presence of banyan trees.
- [Incredible India — Vadodara](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/gujarat/vadodara): Government of India tourism account of Vadapradaka as a village amid banyan trees.
- [Vadodara Municipal Corporation annual report, FY 2021–22](https://vmc.gov.in/pdf/Account/D.%20ANNUAL%20REPORTS/FY%202021-22%20ANNUAL%20REPORT.pdf): VMC’s documented Rajivnagar urban-forest work and parks-and-gardens context.

## Urban forestry, health and biodiversity

- [FAO — Urban and peri-urban forestry](https://www.fao.org/sustainable-forest-management-toolbox/modules/urban-and-peri-urban-forestry/en): planning, species and site suitability, maintenance, monitoring and public participation.
- [FAO — Urban trees: more than just a pretty face](https://www.fao.org/newsroom/story/Urban-trees-more-than-just-a-pretty-face/): urban cooling, air quality and biodiversity context.
- [WHO — Urban green spaces and health](https://www.who.int/europe/publications/i/item/WHO-EURO-2016-3352-43111-60341): evidence pathways linking green space with health and social cohesion.
- [WHO — Urban green-space interventions and health](https://www.who.int/europe/publications/urban-green-space-interventions-and-health--a-review-of-impacts-and-effectiveness.-full-report): evidence that physical improvements paired with social participation can support stronger outcomes.

## Inclusive public space and civic participation

- [UN-Habitat — Public Space](https://unhabitat.org/topic/public-space): public space as social, environmental and civic infrastructure.
- [UN-Habitat — Urban Connections](https://unhabitat.org/project/urban-connections-public-space-planning-for-inclusive-communities-0): participatory planning involving local authorities and communities.
- [World Bank — Handbook for Gender-Inclusive Urban Planning and Design](https://www.worldbank.org/en/topic/urbandevelopment/publication/handbook-for-gender-inclusive-urban-planning-and-design): design and participation involving women and sexual and gender minorities.

## Contact, prejudice and community leadership

- [Pettigrew and Tropp — A meta-analytic test of intergroup contact theory](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16737372/): large meta-analysis finding that intergroup contact typically reduces prejudice, with stronger effects under supportive conditions.
- [UNDP India — Skilling and Livelihoods for Transgender Community](https://www.undp.org/india/publications/skilling-and-livelihoods-transgender-community): barriers, skills and livelihood participation affecting transgender people in India.
- [UNDP India — From the Ground Up](https://www.undp.org/india/stories/ground-how-local-leadership-powering-indias-climate-action): community leadership and environmental stewardship by people often pushed to the margins.

## Editorial safeguards

- Treat all plantation numbers, site names, partner roles and government commitments as unconfirmed until documented by the responsible organisations.
- Use “can”, “may” or “contribute to” for environmental and social outcomes that depend on site, design, maintenance and time.
- Do not prescribe a tree species for a particular site without qualified local assessment.
- Never imply that LGBTQIA+ people must provide public service to deserve unconditional dignity, safety or equal rights.
- Obtain specific consent before identifying or photographing a participant as LGBTQIA+.
